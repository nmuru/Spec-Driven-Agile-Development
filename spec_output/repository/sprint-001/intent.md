# Sprint 1 Goal — Product Discovery and Browsing

Build the first usable version of the ecommerce storefront where a customer can discover products, browse the catalog, search and filter products, view product details and understand available product options.

The sprint should establish the core customer-facing shopping experience and the underlying product/catalog flow needed by later sprints.

The Sprint 1 outcome should allow a customer to:

* Land on the storefront and understand what is being sold.
* Browse featured or available products.
* Browse products by collection/category.
* Search for products.
* Filter or otherwise narrow the product catalog where appropriate.
* Open a product detail page.
* View the product's images, description, price, availability and relevant variants/options.
* Select an available product variant or option where applicable.
* Navigate naturally between the homepage, catalog/collection pages, search results and product pages.
* Use the storefront comfortably on desktop and mobile-sized screens.

Sprint 1 does not need to implement the complete purchasing journey. Cart, checkout, payment, customer accounts and order history are intentionally deferred to later sprints.

The implementation should use the existing repository architecture and available commerce backend/provider capabilities rather than introducing unnecessary replacement infrastructure. Existing technical capabilities and repository conventions should be treated as evidence, but this Intent is the primary statement of what Sprint 1 is intended to accomplish.

The result should be a coherent, demonstrable product-discovery experience that can serve as the foundation for the subsequent shopping and checkout sprint.
