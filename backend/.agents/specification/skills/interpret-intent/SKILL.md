# Interpret Intent

Convert natural-language intent into a faithful working model.

1. Extract desired outcome and observable behaviour.
2. Capture explicit constraints and non-negotiables.
3. Identify exclusions and boundaries.
4. Separate statements, evidence-backed interpretation and unknowns.
5. Preserve ambiguity instead of inventing decisions.

The output is a reasoning aid for specification generation, not a replacement for the user's intent.
