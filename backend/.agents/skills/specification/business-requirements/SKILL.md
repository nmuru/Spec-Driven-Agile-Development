# Business Requirements Skill

Start from the approved Intent and project.md.

1. Identify the users, desired outcomes, and meaningful behavior in the requested increment.
2. Express that behavior as concise user stories.
3. Add acceptance criteria that are observable and testable.
4. Preserve explicit boundaries and exclusions from Intent.
5. Mark genuine ambiguity instead of inventing stakeholder decisions.

Do not design the solution or decompose implementation tasks.
